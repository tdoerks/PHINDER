# Citations

If you use PHINDER, please cite the pipeline and the tools it integrates.

## Pipeline framework

- **Nextflow**: Di Tommaso P, et al. Nextflow enables reproducible computational workflows. *Nat Biotechnol.* 2017;35(4):316-319. doi:10.1038/nbt.3820

## Quality control & assembly

- **FastQC**: Andrews S. FastQC: a quality control tool for high throughput sequence data. https://www.bioinformatics.babraham.ac.uk/projects/fastqc/
- **fastp**: Chen S, et al. fastp: an ultra-fast all-in-one FASTQ preprocessor. *Bioinformatics.* 2018;34(17):i884-i890. doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/bty560
- **SPAdes**: Bankevich A, et al. SPAdes: a new genome assembly algorithm and its applications to single-cell sequencing. *J Comput Biol.* 2012;19(5):455-477. doi:10.1089/cmb.2012.0021
- **QUAST**: Gurevich A, et al. QUAST: quality assessment tool for genome assemblies. *Bioinformatics.* 2013;29(8):1072-1075. doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/btt086

## Quality assessment

- **CheckV**: Nayfach S, et al. CheckV assesses the quality and completeness of metagenome-assembled viral genomes. *Nat Biotechnol.* 2021;39(5):578-585. doi:10.1038/s41587-020-00774-7

- **geNomad**: Camargo AP, et al. Identification of mobile genetic elements with geNomad. *Nat Biotechnol.* 2024;42(8):1303-1312. doi:10.1038/s41587-023-01953-y
